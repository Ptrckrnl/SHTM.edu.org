About the school

Learn the industry by working in it

We prepare students for leadership roles in the global hospitality and tourism industry.

Students learn by doing, from practice in school labs to training in real workplaces.

  1. Lab practice

    Kitchen work, food and beverage service, front office, and housekeeping in school training areas.

  2. Events and tours

    Students plan and run school events and tour activities.

  3. On-the-job training

    Supervised training in hotels, restaurants, and travel and tour companies.

  4. Industry partners

    Real work experience with partner businesses, with some international exposure.

FAQ

Common questions

Quick answers for students and parents. Still curious? Send us a message.

What programs does SHTM offer?

Two four-year degrees: Bachelor of Science in Hospitality Management (BSHM) and Bachelor of Science in Tourism Management (BSTM).

What is the difference between BSHM and BSTM?

BSHM focuses on hotels, restaurants, food and beverage service, and events. BSTM focuses on travel and tour operations, destinations, sustainable tourism, and tourism marketing.

Will I train outside the school?

Yes. Students take supervised on-the-job training in hotels, restaurants, and travel and tour companies, after practicing in the school's training labs.

How do I ask about admission requirements?

Send an inquiry through the contact form or email shtm@southland.edu.ph. The department office will reply with the requirements.

Where is the office and when is it open?

The SHTM Office is at Southland College, Kabankalan City, Negros Occidental. It is open Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM.
